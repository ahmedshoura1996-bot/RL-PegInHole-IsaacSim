# M3.2 Investigation — Peg-in-Hole Fixture and Collision Validation

## Objective

Validate the peg-in-hole fixture geometry, collision setup, fixture placement,
and the observed peg stopping behavior before modifying the physics parameters.

## Environment Geometry

- Peg radius: 0.010 m
- Peg diameter: 0.020 m = 20 mm
- Hole radius: 0.0105 m
- Hole diameter: 0.021 m = 21 mm
- Diameter clearance: 0.001 m = 1 mm
- Fixture width: 0.080 m
- Fixture depth: 0.080 m
- Fixture height: 0.020 m
- Side width: 0.0295 m

## Fixture Expected Positions

- Left:  (0.474750, 0.000000, 0.010000)
- Right: (0.525250, 0.000000, 0.010000)
- Front: (0.500000, -0.025250, 0.010000)
- Back:  (0.500000,  0.025250, 0.010000)

## Runtime Fixture Transform Validation

The fixture root transforms were inspected at runtime.

Observed:

- LEFT:  [0.47475, 0.0, 0.01]
- RIGHT: [0.52525, 0.0, 0.01]
- FRONT: [0.5, -0.02525, 0.01]
- BACK: [0.5, 0.02525, 0.01]

The runtime values match the analytically expected positions.

### Conclusion

The fixture root-level transforms are correct and are not considered the
primary cause of the observed peg stopping behavior.

## Collision Validation

Runtime inspection confirmed collision descendants for:

- Object/geometry/mesh — Cylinder
- FixtureLeft/geometry/mesh — Cube
- FixtureRight/geometry/mesh — Cube
- FixtureFront/geometry/mesh — Cube
- FixtureBack/geometry/mesh — Cube

Collision configuration was enabled for the peg and all four fixtures.

## Center-Drop Test

The peg was initialized above the center of the hole.

Observed behavior:

- Peg descends under gravity.
- Peg remains centered approximately at X=0.5, Y=0.0.
- Peg stops at approximately Z=0.022 m.
- The peg does not continue through the 21 mm hole despite the 20 mm peg diameter.

## Current Investigation Status

Confirmed:

1. Fixture dimensions are mathematically consistent.
2. Peg diameter is smaller than hole diameter.
3. Four fixture objects exist in the scene.
4. Four fixture collision descendants exist.
5. Fixture root transforms are correct at runtime.
6. Peg gravity/fall behavior is active.
7. Center-drop still stops at approximately Z=0.022 m.

## Next Investigation

Inspect the local transform and scale of the actual collision geometry
(`geometry/mesh`) rather than only the fixture root transforms.

The goal is to determine whether the collision mesh dimensions and local/world
transforms match the configured Cuboid dimensions.

No physics/contact parameters have been modified at this stage.
